Magic Knights

The Magic Knights are magical warriors who serve and protect the Clover Kingdom. They use grimoires and magical abilities to complete missions and fight dangerous enemies.

The organization consists of nine squads, each led by a captain. Every squad has its own personality, fighting style and powerful members.

🐂 Black Bulls

The Black Bulls are one of the most memorable squads in Black Clover. Although they are considered troublesome, their members possess incredible magical abilities and repeatedly prove themselves in dangerous battles.

Their captain is Yami Sukehiro , a powerful Dark Magic user.
