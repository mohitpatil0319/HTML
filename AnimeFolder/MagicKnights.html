Magic Knights

The Magic Knights are powerful magical warriors who protect the Clover Kingdom from enemies, monsters, and other threats. They use their magical abilities and grimoires to fight and protect innocent people. The Magic Knights are divided into several squads, and each squad is led by a captain.

There are nine Magic Knight squads in the Clover Kingdom. Each squad has its own captain, vice-captains, and members. The squads include the Black Bulls, Golden Dawn, Silver Eagles, Crimson Lion Kings, Blue Rose, Green Praying Mantises, Coral Peacocks, Purple Orcas, and Aqua Deer.

The Black Bulls are one of the most unusual Magic Knight squads. They are led by Captain Yami Sukehiro and include members such as Asta, Noelle Silva, Magna Swing, Luck Voltia, Finral Roulacase, and Vanessa Enoteca. Although the members have different personalities and abilities, they work together as a team.

Magic Knights can improve their rank by completing missions and showing their skills. Their main purpose is to protect the Clover Kingdom and its people. Becoming a Magic Knight is also an important step for people who dream of becoming the Wizard King.
