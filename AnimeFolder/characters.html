Meet some of the most powerful and memorable characters from Black Clover.

Main Characters

Asta

Main Protagonist

Asta is a boy born without magic. He dreams of becoming the Wizard King and fights using powerful Anti-Magic.

Anti-Magic

Yuno

Asta's Rival

Yuno is Asta's childhood friend and rival. He possesses a four-leaf grimoire and uses powerful Wind Magic.

Wind Magic

Yami Sukehiro

Captain — Black Bulls

Yami is the captain of the Black Bulls. He uses Dark Magic and pushes his squad members beyond their limits.

Dark Magic

Noelle Silva

Black Bulls

Noelle is a royal member of House Silva. She gradually learns to control her powerful Water Magic.

Water Magic

William Vangeance

Captain — Golden Dawn

William is the captain of the Golden Dawn and a highly respected Magic Knight. He uses powerful World Tree Magic.

World Tree Magic

Mereoleona Vermillion

Crimson Lion

Mereoleona is an incredibly powerful warrior and a master of Fire Magic. She is known for her fearless fighting style.

Fire Magic
