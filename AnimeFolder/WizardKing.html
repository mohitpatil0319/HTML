About Julius

Julius Novachrono is the 28th Wizard King of the Clover Kingdom in Black Clover. He is one of the most powerful and respected magic users in the kingdom.

Julius has an incredible fascination with different types of magic. He often disguises himself so that he can explore the kingdom and observe unique magical abilities.

Despite being the Wizard King, Julius is friendly, curious and carefree. He believes that people should not be judged by their social status or magical abilities.

Time Magic

Julius can manipulate time, allowing him to accelerate, slow down, stop and store time.

⚡

Time Steal

Julius can steal time from his opponents and use the stored time to power his abilities.

🔮

Time Storage

He can store time inside his grimoire and use it later when necessary.

🌌

Mana Zone

Julius can control mana over a large area, giving him exceptional battlefield awareness.

🌀

Chrono Anastasis

A powerful Time Magic spell that allows Julius to manipulate the flow of time across a wide area.

Transformation Magic
