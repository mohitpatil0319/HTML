The Story

Black Clover is an anime about Asta, a boy born without magic in a world where magic is everything.

Despite having no magical power, Asta dreams of becoming the Wizard King, the strongest and most respected Magic Knight in the Clover Kingdom.

After receiving a mysterious five-leaf grimoire containing Anti-Magic, Asta joins the Black Bulls and begins an incredible journey alongside his rival and childhood friend, Yuno.

Together with their friends, Asta and Yuno face powerful enemies, dangerous magical threats and challenges that push them beyond their limits.
